# FOI request: LGPS employer contributions, 2017-18 to 2026-27

Replace the bracketed fields. A blank CSV in the requested layout is in
`foi/FOI_response_template.csv` and can be attached to the request.

---

**To:** Freedom of Information Officer, [Council name]
**Subject:** Freedom of Information request – LGPS employer contributions, 2017-18 to 2026-27

Dear Freedom of Information Officer,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, please provide the following
information about [Council name]'s staff in the Local Government Pension
Scheme (LGPS), including staff in the council's maintained schools, for each
financial year from 2017-18 to 2026-27. For 2026-27, please give the rate
currently being paid and the figures to date, and state the date they run to.

1. The total employer contributions the council actually paid to the LGPS (£).
   Please include all regular employer contributions, both percentage-of-pay
   contributions and any fixed lump sums. Please exclude employee
   contributions and one-off early retirement or ill-health costs.
2. Total pensionable pay of LGPS members (£).
3. The number of LGPS members at 31 March (for 2026-27, the latest date held).
4. The number of employees who were eligible for the LGPS but had opted out,
   at the same date.

If different employer contribution rates applied to different groups of staff
(for example council staff and school staff), please give items 1 to 4
separately for each group, with the rate that applied to it.

Please provide this as a CSV or spreadsheet with these columns, one row per
financial year per group:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Financial_year | e.g. 2023-24 |
| Staff_group | "All staff", or the group name if rates differ between groups |
| Employer_rate_pct | Employer contribution rate applied to this group's pensionable pay (%) |
| Employer_contributions_paid_GBP | Item 1 |
| Pensionable_pay_GBP | Item 2 |
| LGPS_members | Item 3 |
| Opted_out_employees | Item 4 |
| Figures_as_at | Date the figures run to (31 March, or the latest date for 2026-27) |

If this would exceed the cost limit, please provide items 1 and 2 for all
years first, then items 3 and 4. If some years are not held, please provide
the years that are.

Yours faithfully,

[Name]
[Organisation]
[Email address]

---

*Notes for the sender (not part of the request): send to the council itself,
not its pension fund. Wales is covered by the same Act; Scotland is not.*
