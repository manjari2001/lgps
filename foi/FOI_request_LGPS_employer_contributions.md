# FOI request: LGPS employer contributions, 2017-18 to 2026-27

Template for sending to each council (as the employer, not the pension fund).
Replace the bracketed fields. The request is ordered so that, if the council
says it would exceed the cost limit, the most important parts come first
(see "If this exceeds the cost limit").

---

**To:** Freedom of Information Officer, [Council name]
**Subject:** Freedom of Information request – Local Government Pension Scheme employer contributions, 2017-18 to 2026-27

Dear Freedom of Information Officer,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, please provide the following
information about [Council name]'s contributions, as an employer, to the
Local Government Pension Scheme (LGPS), through [Pension fund name].

Please cover each financial year from **2017-18 to 2026-27 inclusive**
(10 years). For 2026-27, which is still in progress, please give the rates
payable under the Rates and Adjustments Certificate and the budgeted or
forecast amounts, and state the date to which any actual figures run.

This request covers the LGPS only. It does not cover the Teachers' Pension
Scheme or the Firefighters' Pension Schemes.

**1. Contribution rates payable (per financial year)**

a) The employer's primary contribution rate (% of pensionable pay).
b) The secondary contribution, stated as a percentage of pay, a cash sum
   (£), or both, exactly as set out in the Rates and Adjustments Certificate.
c) The total contribution rate, and, where the secondary is a cash sum, any
   equivalent total rate as a percentage of pay that the council or the
   actuary has calculated.
d) The employer code(s) and the name of each certificate line these rates
   apply to (for example "Council (excluding schools)", "LEA schools pool").

**2. Contributions actually paid (per financial year, £)**

a) Employer contributions paid on pensionable pay (the percentage element).
b) Secondary or deficit contributions paid as cash sums.
c) Any contributions paid in advance (prepayments). For each, please give the
   amount, the year(s) it covers, and any discount applied.
d) Separately, and not included in (a) to (c): early retirement strain costs,
   ill-health costs and any other one-off payments to the fund.

If the rates actually paid in any year differ from those in the certificate
(for example because of a prepayment or an agreed change), please say so.

**3. Pensionable pay and membership (per financial year)**

a) Total pensionable pay (£) on which the employer contributions in 2(a)
   were calculated.
b) The number of active LGPS members at 31 March (or the nearest date held).
c) If held, the number of employees who were eligible but not members
   (opted out), or the council's LGPS participation rate.

**4. Whether contributions differ by pay or by staff group**

a) Please confirm whether the employer contribution rate was the same
   percentage of pensionable pay for all members, or whether it differed by
   pay level, staff group, pool or employer code. If it differed, please give
   the rates for each group and the pensionable pay each applies to.
b) Employee contributions in the LGPS are tiered by pay band. For each
   financial year, please provide, for each employee contribution band:
   the pay range, the employee contribution rate, the number of members, and
   their total pensionable pay (and total employee contributions, if held).
   Please show members in the 50/50 section separately if held.

**5. Scope of the figures**

For each year, please state whether the figures include:
a) staff in maintained schools (community, voluntary controlled, voluntary
   aided and foundation schools), and whether school staff are in the same
   certificate line as the council's other staff or a separate one;
b) any other bodies whose contributions are paid through the council's
   employer code (for example a council-owned company or a pooled employer).

**Format**

I would be grateful to receive this as a spreadsheet (Excel or CSV), with one
row per financial year and, where relevant, per certificate line or pay
band. Figures already published in a form that does not require further
work are fine. If you rely on section 21 (information reasonably accessible
by other means), please give the exact document and page for each item,
since statements of accounts do not usually separate these elements.

**If this exceeds the cost limit**

If answering in full would exceed the cost limit under section 12, please
tell me which parts can be answered within it, per your duty to advise
and assist under section 16. In priority order:
1. Items 1(a)–(c) and 3(a), all years
2. Items 2(a)–(c), all years
3. Item 4
4. The remaining items

If some years are not held (for example before a system change), please
provide the years that are held and say which are not.

Thank you for your help. Please contact me if anything in this request is
unclear.

Yours faithfully,

[Name]
[Organisation]
[Email address]

## Notes for whoever sends these (not part of the request)

- **Send to the council, not the fund.** The council holds its own payroll and the split between council and school staff. Funds hold contribution totals, but in metropolitan and Welsh funds they cover several councils and may treat requests as out of scope.
- **Why item 4 asks both ways.** In the LGPS the employer normally pays one percentage of pensionable pay per certificate line; it is employee contributions that vary by pay band. Item 4(a) confirms this for each council and catches exceptions (separate pools or rates for schools). Item 4(b) gives the employee pay-band profile, which also lets member contributions be converted back into payroll accurately, the main weakness of the fund-annual-report benchmarks in phase 5.
- **Items 1(c) and 3(a)** are what phase 5 is missing. They turn every "X% plus £Y" total into an exact percentage, and give ground truth to check the RO + CFR estimates against.
- **Timing:** 20 working days is the statutory deadline. The 2017-18 to 2019-20 years span a system change for many councils, so expect some "not held" answers for the early years.
- **Scotland** is out of scope (different Act and scheme); Wales is covered by the same Act.
